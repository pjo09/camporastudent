Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Campora Technologies ("Campora", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of students, property owners, and platform visitors.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and password when you register as a student or PG owner.
  • Booking & Saved Preferences: Accommodation search preferences, saved properties, and booking transaction details.
  • Property Owner Listings: Property details, address, rental rates, photos, and contact preferences.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and session tokens strictly necessary for secure authentication.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and personalize the Campora student housing marketplace.
  • To process booking requests, saved properties, and owner notifications.
  • To enforce Row-Level Security (RLS) and protect user account access.
  • To communicate essential updates regarding your account, bookings, or inquiry responses.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard database encryption and strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies on our PostgreSQL & Supabase infrastructure. Your private account data, messages, and bookings are isolated so that users can only access their own authorized information.

4. Third-Party Services

Campora integrates with trusted third-party providers including Supabase Auth for authentication and Cloudinary/Resend for media storage and transactional communications. We do not sell your personal information to third-party advertisers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your profile data at any time via your account settings page or by contacting support@campora.in.
