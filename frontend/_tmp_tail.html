How Campora works

Three steps, zero guesswork.

01 / TELL US

Drop your campus, not your address

Search by university or college and we map every stay around it — no scrolling through a whole city.

02 / COMPARE

Compare what actually matters

Walk time to class, verified safety checks, real rent — laid out side by side, not buried in a listing.

03 / LOCK IT IN

Book your room online

Pay a secure token, sign digitally, and your room is held — before someone else clicks first.
