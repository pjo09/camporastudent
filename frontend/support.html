FAQ

How do I book a property?

Browse properties in Explore, open a property and tap Book Now, choose dates and submit. The owner will confirm your booking.

How does payment work?

Payments are processed securely via Razorpay after your booking is accepted. Receipts appear under Payments & Documents.

How do I raise a maintenance request?

Go to Maintenance, tap New Request, choose the booked property and describe the issue. Owners get notified instantly.

How do I contact my owner?

Open Messages, select a conversation, and chat directly with the property owner.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, from My Bookings tap Cancel. Bookings that already started cannot be cancelled.
