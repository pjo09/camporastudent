About Campora

India's dedicated student accommodation platform connecting college students with verified PGs, hostels, and shared flats.

Our Mission

Finding safe, affordable, and transparent student housing near campus should be straightforward and stress-free. Campora provides a digital marketplace designed specifically for students, offering verified property listings, upfront pricing, transparent house rules, and seamless online booking requests.

What We Provide

  • Verified Listings: Accommodation options checked for essential amenities, security, and student eligibility.
  • Direct Contact: Transparent communication between students and property owners.
  • Zero Hidden Surprises: Clear information regarding rent, security deposits, maintenance fees, and house rules.
  • Student-Centric Search: Filter by proximity to major universities, gender eligibility, sharing preferences, and budget.

For Property Owners

Campora empowers PG and hostel owners with modern listing tools, booking management, and resident verification requests to maintain vibrant, trustworthy student communities.
